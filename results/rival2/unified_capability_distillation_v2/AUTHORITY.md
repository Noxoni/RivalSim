# Rival 2 Unified Capability Distillation V2 Authority

V2 is the prospective correction to the completed V1 negative result.  It still
produces one recurrent policy with no runtime router or task identifier.

The V23 trunk and critic are byte-identical in the Capability V2 checkpoints.
V2 therefore initializes the frozen base actor to the elementwise mean of the
immutable Capability V2 Blue and Orange actor heads.  This bakes the shared
demo/recovery actor delta into the policy rather than asking the recurrent path
to resolve a sub-0.0004 target concurrently with the much larger aerial delta.
The recurrent residual remains zero-initialized and learns Ground-to-Air Goal
V3 while all ordinary and capability families are rehearsed.

V1 train/validation results are diagnostic only.  V2 uses new deterministic
corpus seeds, a new complete validation corpus, and test seeds that remain
untouched until one validation checkpoint is selected.  Selection permits a
small bounded action deviation from each side-specific Capability V2 teacher
because one canonical actor cannot be exactly equal to both distinct heads.

This authority permits supervised consolidation and direct physical evaluation
only.  It does not permit PPO, reward changes, policy routing, or promotion over
the existing official build.
