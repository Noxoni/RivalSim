Concessions fell 25%, with fewer concessions in nine paired matches and equal
concessions in one. Contacts were essentially flat, slightly lower. The same-player
follow-up fraction rose from 3.27% to 9.28%, but Nexto still made 215 of the next 237
resolved contacts. This is better contact follow-through, not sustained possession
or effective offense. One +200 contact was censored at match end.

Forward displacement means canonical net ball-y movement exceeding 100 uu before
the next contact or goal. Contact velocity diagnostics measure velocity CHANGE,
not the resulting velocity or shot quality. The one-contact kickoff statistic is
not a time window. Demolition counts do not establish tactical intent. No aerial,
dash, possession-duration, or named-mechanic capability is inferred here.

All ten matches ran exactly 36,000 physics ticks. Every goal entry is valid, native
goal counts equal recurrent reset counts, there was no goal-buffer overflow, and
both checkpoint bytes and model weights were unchanged. The worker completed in
117.236 seconds. `full_match_000200_integrity.json` is generated by the CPU-only
`benchmarks/report_rival2_ssl_entity_continuation_match.py`; it verifies those
invariants and reduces existing native counters without new simulation/detectors.
Its PASS means evidence integrity, not gameplay acceptance.
