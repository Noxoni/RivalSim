Parent-to-final paired goal difference improved in five worlds and worsened in
five, but aggregate scoring, contacts and repeated-contact fraction were worse.
The isolated +5 kickoff first contact did not persist. This arm has not shown
that the corrected first-contact lesson transfers to competitive standing
kickoffs, nor that shooting or possession improved. It does not prove that a
longer run could never learn; it supplies no basis for claiming it already did.

Repeated contact means the same player made the next distinct native contact,
not sustained possession. Kickoff counts include goal restarts, not only the ten
initial starts. No-touch resets are disabled by evaluation protocol; their zero
count is not evidence of competence. Goal-directed velocity change is not a
verified shot. No named-mechanic competence is inferred here.
