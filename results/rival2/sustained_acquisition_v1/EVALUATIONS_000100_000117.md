# Full-match regression at 100; acquisition probes at 107 and 117

These are completed, read-only development evaluations. No reward, policy,
optimizer, exploration, curriculum or evaluation setting was changed for this
publication. The healthy trainer was not interrupted or overlapped with additional
GPU work. The user's stationary-kickoff correction remains mandatory.

## Full-match Nexto evaluation: update 100

The same ten-match specification as update 50 was used: five genuine stationary
kickoff layouts, both Rival sides, deterministic Rival argmax, active native-v5
Nexto, 300 seconds of regulation per match, native goal resets, and no training
inactivity resets. All ten matches completed regulation without overtime.

| Metric | Previously reported 50 | Newly completed 100 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Rival wins / matches | 0 / 10 | 0 / 10 |
| Rival goals / conceded | 0 / 335 | 0 / 523 |
| Rival native contacts | 151 | 4 |
| Rival contacts per minute | 3.02 | 0.08 |
| Entirely touchless matches | 0 | 7 |
| Kickoff first contacts | 0 | 0 |
| Rival followups / resolved followups | 1 / 149 | 0 / 4 |

This is a severe gameplay regression, not evidence of successful transfer. Rival
made only four contacts across 50 aggregate regulation minutes. Nexto made the next
contact after each one. Nexto recorded 1,484 contacts. All four Rival contacts
increased goalward ball velocity, but subsequent displacement was forward once,
backward twice and neutral once. Four favorable instantaneous velocity changes are
not sustained control or scoring. There were zero Rival demolition events.

No-touch truncations are zero by evaluation protocol, not evidence of healthy
acquisition; seven entirely touchless matches are the relevant negative result.
The raw goal records, scoreboard, scorer identities and reset bookkeeping agree.
Integrity PASS means a valid recorded evaluation, not gameplay PASS or proof that
all implementation paths are fault-free. This comparison does not establish a
cause for the regression, including whether the stationary-start training amendment
contributed. Full-match evaluation kickoffs were stationary before and after it.

## Separate acquisition development probes

Same 1,024 held-out-generation starts, active Nexto, alternating easy/varied cases,
native own-contact evidence in the original episode. Easy deadline is 5 seconds;
varied deadline is 8 seconds. Rival need not contact before Nexto. Neither a touch
nor a successful probe implies useful possession, a won kickoff or a scored goal.

| Metric | Previously reported 97 | New 107 | New 117 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Easy contacts within 5 s | 293/512 (57.2265625%) | 329/512 (64.2578125%) | 315/512 (61.5234375%) |
| Varied contacts within 8 s | 176/512 (34.375%) | 181/512 (35.3515625%) | 230/512 (44.921875%) |
| Easy median first contact, successes only | 0.641667 s | 0.691667 s | 0.650000 s |
| Varied median first contact, successes only | 1.233333 s | 1.291667 s | 1.200000 s |

At 117, easy successes decreased by 14 from 107, while varied successes increased
by 49. Both exceed 97, but acquisition remains unreliable and the frozen 95%/90%
retirement thresholds are not met. The retirement streak remains zero. Conditional
medians compare different success sets and do not establish same-state acceleration.
Failures are retained as -1 in the complete first-contact tick arrays.

The later probes do not reevaluate full matches at 107 or 117. They cannot be used
to claim that update 100's match regression has recovered. Controlled near-ball
contact learning and real-match performance are currently giving different results.
Recommendation: investigate the transfer gap before making further capability or
training changes; do not treat probe gains as a substitute for full-match results.

## Actual training exposure, not source-bank percentages

The closed prefix through 117 retains all per-update family/opponent telemetry.
Across updates 98-117, total exposure was 1,966,080 world-seconds. Acquisition starts
accounted for 1,087,314.333324 world-seconds (55.3036668563%), versus the nominal
50% reset-bank share. The split was 561,079.666662 acquisition/Nexto world-seconds
and 526,234.666662 acquisition/self-play world-seconds. Different episode durations
make reset-bank percentage and realized frame exposure different quantities.

## Integrity and immutable identities

Both original and supplementary stationary-kickoff authorities were reverified
against local hashes and origin/main. Every published checkpoint passed the CPU
lineage, contracts, continuous-update-prefix, exposure, finite model/Adam and Adam
counter audit. Each carries the exact stationary-kickoff amendment from update 76.
No KL rejection was introduced. Checkpoint learner decisions: 442,368,000 at 100;
473,333,760 at 107; 517,570,560 at 117.

The full-match reducer was rerun on the saved raw result, with exact equality to
the saved integrity artifact. All eleven integrity checks passed. Probe counts,
fractions and conditional medians were independently recomputed from all 1,024
first-contact tick values on CPU. Result/receipt checkpoint hashes match actual
checkpoint bytes. All evaluations record unchanged model, checkpoint and Nexto,
with zero optimizer steps. No live evaluation was repeated for publication.
