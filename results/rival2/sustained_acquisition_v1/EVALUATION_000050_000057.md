The contact rate is about 7.95 times the prior result and concessions are about
45.1% lower. This comparison spans training before and after the acquisition
amendment; it is not an isolated causal ablation of that amendment.

Results are still poor: Rival lost all ten matches without scoring and never got
the first kickoff contact. Of 149 resolved post-Rival-contact followups, only one
was another Rival contact and 148 were Nexto contacts. This is not a possession-
duration measure, but it does not support a claim of reliable sustained control.
One native Rival demolition was recorded; it is not evidence of a demo strategy.
148 contacts improved canonical goalward ball velocity, but subsequent measured
ball displacement was forward in 44 cases, backward in 59, neutral in 46. Neither
velocity improvement nor a contact is automatically a good shot.

All saved evaluation integrity checks passed, including ten complete matches,
score/event conservation, native resets, model/checkpoint equality and zero
optimizer steps. Nexto weights were also unchanged. The existing CPU reducer was
used to verify the saved raw event evidence.
