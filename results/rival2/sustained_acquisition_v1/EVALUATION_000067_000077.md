# Completed acquisition probes 67 and 77

These two previously unreported development probes used the exact same fixed
1,024-state bank, evaluation specification, deterministic Rival controller and
active native-v5 Nexto as the already reported probe57. The healthy resumed trainer
was not interrupted for this CPU-only publication.

| Metric | Already reported57 | New67 | New77 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Easy contact within5s | 294/512 (57.421875%) | 304/512 (59.375%) | 286/512 (55.859375%) |
| Varied contact within8s | 118/512 (23.046875%) | 128/512 (25%) | 91/512 (17.7734375%) |
| Easy median first contact, successful attempts only | 0.600s | 0.600s | 0.600s |
| Varied median first contact, successful attempts only | 1.125s | 1.125s | 1.100s |

Both groups improved from57 to67. At77, easy successes dropped by18 and varied
successes by37 relative to67. Both also fell below57. This is a measured regression
on the repeated acquisition probe, not continued monotonic improvement. A slightly
lower median among a smaller successful subset does not offset the lost successes
or prove faster same-state acquisition. Every missed original attempt is retained.

Neither result qualifies for the unchanged95%easy/90%varied retirement threshold;
the streak remains zero and temporary acquisition starts remain active. The probe
does not require a good follow-up, possession, a shot or a goal and is not a kickoff
test. It cannot establish full-match or SSL performance.

## Important training boundary

Probe67 occurred before the user's stationary-kickoff correction. Training stopped
at76 and resumed the identical model and Adam with new physical episodes and zeroed
histories, as documented. Only the final update leading to77 used the corrected
kickoff source bank. Acquisition evaluation inputs themselves did not change.
The67-to77 comparison spans nine updates before the correction and one after it;
it does not isolate the cause of the drop and is not evidence for reverting the
user's correction. No reward, PPO, exploration or curriculum setting was changed
in response to these results. The scheduled full-match evaluation remains separate.
